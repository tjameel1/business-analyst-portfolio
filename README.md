# Digital Banking Adoption & Customer Segmentation

**MS Business Analytics Capstone | Kent State University**

## Business Problem
Financial institutions need to understand which customer characteristics and behaviors are associated with mobile banking adoption to improve customer engagement and digital service strategies.

## Project Objectives
- Identify patterns associated with mobile banking adoption.
- Compare predictive classification models.
- Segment customers based on shared characteristics.
- Develop actionable business recommendations.

## Tools & Methods
R, R Markdown, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, K-Means Clustering, Data Cleaning, Predictive Analytics

## Key Results
- Decision Tree: 97.6% test accuracy.
- Logistic Regression: approximately 87.8% accuracy.
- K-Means: four customer clusters identified.

## Business Recommendations
Explore targeted digital onboarding, customer education, and segment-specific engagement strategies. Monitor digital adoption rates and active mobile banking usage to evaluate effectiveness.

## Limitations
Findings are based on an academic survey dataset and require further validation before operational deployment.
